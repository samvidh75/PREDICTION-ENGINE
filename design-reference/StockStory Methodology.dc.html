A StockEX score is the weighted mean of five factor scores. Each factor is a percentile against the PSE universe, not an absolute grade — a 90 means top decile on the exchange, nothing more.

03 What the states mean

A state describes the direction of the evidence since we last reviewed the company. It is deliberately not a recommendation, and it says nothing about whether the price already reflects it.

04 Coverage, not confidence

Each company shows a coverage figure. It measures how much of the required input set we actually found in primary filings — it is a completeness measure, never a verdict on quality.
