Trust & Methodology

What StockEX Is

StockEX is a structured equity research platform. We provide scorecards, theses, risk assessments, scenario analysis, and peer comparisons for listed PSX equities.

What StockEX Is Not

We do not provide portfolio management services. We are not a trading platform or brokerage.

Research-Only Policy

Every feature on StockEX is designed for research purposes. Scorecards are for relative comparison within our framework.

Data Sources & Accuracy

We aggregate data from exchange filings, regulatory databases, and market data sources. All data is timestamped and attributed. We do not guarantee data accuracy — users should verify critical data points independently.

Conflicts of Interest

StockEX does not accept compensation from listed companies for research. We do not engage in paid promotions of stocks.
