Why we built it
MR
Miguel Reyes
Co-founder · Makati City

I spent four years reading PSE disclosures on a Sunday night because nobody else was going to do it for me.

The filings are all public. They are also PDFs, posted at odd hours, written for regulators. If you hold eight or ten names, keeping up is a weekend job — and the week you skip is usually the week something moves.

So we built the thing we wanted: something that reads every filing as it lands, remembers what we thought last quarter, and says plainly when the two no longer agree. Not a score out of ten pretending to be certain. A sentence a person can argue with.

We do not take orders, hold your money, or tell you what to buy. We are not a broker and have no interest in becoming one. When you have made up your mind, you go to the broker you already use.
